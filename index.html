MAZE RUNNER

Maze runner is a dystopian novel series about an alternate world where global warming disasters and overpopulation drive the government to release a man made virus that eventually mutates into a sort of zombie infection and to solve it they make use of the new generation of immune children to potentially find a cure by putting them through often deadly trials to stimulate their brains to get a full map and why the virus has no effect.

With a total of 8 books.
There are five in the first series and three in the second.

Series ONE

  • The Maze Runner
  • The Scorch Trials
  • The Death Cure
  • The Kill Order

The fifth book, called 'Crank Palace' is a sort of mini book that follows a non immune character after their choice of leaving their friend group of immunes during the book of:
'The Death Cure to avoid hurting them when they become infected.

Another note, if put in chronological order, the book: The Kill Order would be first.
As its a seperate set of characters taking place before and right after the virus is begining to mutate with some background of what was happening to the world itself before the trails and immune children were taken.

Series TWO

  • The Maze Cutter
  • The Godhead Complex
  • The Infinite Glade
    •

These three books take place around 73 years after the events of: The Death Cure where the original characters had escaped onto a remote island away from civilization to sort of restart from scratch from all the immunes.
Following the generations that came from the surviving immunes as they venture back to the main land by the request of a traveler that seeked them out from the new civillizations that appeared.
The Islanders, The Remenent Nation, and The Godhead.
