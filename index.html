MAZE RUNNER

Maze runner is a dystopian novel series about an alternate world where global warming disasters and overpopulation drive the government to release a man made virus that eventually mutates and to solve it they make use of the new generation of immune children to potentially find a cure by putting them through often deadly trials to stimulate their brains to get a full map and why the virus has no effect.

With a total of 8 books.
There are five in the first series and three in the second.
